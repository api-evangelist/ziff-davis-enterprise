IPVanish has just made it easier to take agency over your online privacy by introducing a dedicated in-app RAM-only filter across all apps. The tool lets you easily find and connect to its growing network of RAM-only servers. By enabling the filter, you can browse available connection points by country, region, and city, instantly spotting which clusters have already been upgraded to the diskless infrastructure.
